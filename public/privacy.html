Website privacy

This notice covers the Tiny Oak Lab studio website. The products linked from it have their own privacy notices.

Visiting the site

The studio site uses no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. Its hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the site.

Project inquiries

The inquiry form opens a draft in your email app. The site does not receive or store the draft. If you send an email, Tiny Oak Lab uses the information you share to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project.

External links

Google Play and YouTube are external services. Their own terms and privacy policies apply when you open those links.
